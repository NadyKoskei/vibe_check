About Me

The Human Behind the Code

KN

Hey, I'm Koskei Nady — a frontend developer based in Nairobi with a soft spot for type scales, motion design, and interfaces that stay fast on mid-range Android devices.

I started out tweaking MySpace themes at 14 and never really stopped. Today I spend my days turning design files into accessible, production-ready components, and my evenings contributing to open source or sketching side-project ideas in a very battered notebook.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me chasing good coffee, shooting street photography, or getting unfairly competitive at board game night.
